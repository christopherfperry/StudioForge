Fitzrovia Records DAW @VERSION@ is installed

Open Applications › Fitzrovia Records DAW (or search “Fitzrovia Records DAW” in Spotlight).

On first launch a welcome screen asks for audio-input access. Click Allow Audio Input… and then OK so you can record. You can change this later in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone or under Help › Setup & Permissions.

Getting started: the New Project window opens: pick a template, name your project and click Create. Then arm a track (R button) and press R to record. To separate an existing song into stems, click Split (click Download AI Model there once for the best results). Mute (M), solo (S) and volume are in each track header and in the Mixer.

Your library is in Music › Fitzrovia Records DAW. Choose your audio interface and buffer size in Audio Settings (the sliders icon in the transport bar).
