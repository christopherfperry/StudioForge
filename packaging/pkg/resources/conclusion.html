Fitzrovia Records DAW is installed

Open Applications › Fitzrovia Records DAW (or search “Fitzrovia Records DAW” in Spotlight).

On first launch macOS will ask whether Fitzrovia Records DAW may use the microphone. Click OK so you can record. You can change this later in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.

Your library is in Music › Fitzrovia Records DAW. Choose your audio interface and buffer size in Fitzrovia Records DAW's Audio Settings.
