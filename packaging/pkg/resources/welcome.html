Welcome to Fitzrovia Records DAW

This installer will:

  • Install Fitzrovia Records DAW.app into your Applications folder.
  • Create your library in Music › Fitzrovia Records DAW (Projects, Recordings, Exports, Presets, Impulse Responses, Models, Samples).
  • Copy the built-in presets into Music › Fitzrovia Records DAW › Presets › Factory.
  • Register Fitzrovia Records DAW so double-clicking a .sfproj project opens it.

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later. About 50 MB of disk space; the optional stem-separation model (1–2 GB) is downloaded later from inside the app.
