Welcome to Fitzrovia Records DAW @VERSION@

This installer will:

  • Install Fitzrovia Records DAW.app into your Applications folder (an older copy is upgraded; a copy named StudioForge.app is replaced).
  • Create your library in Music › Fitzrovia Records DAW (Projects, Recordings, Exports, Presets, Impulse Responses, Models, Samples).
  • Copy the built-in presets into Music › Fitzrovia Records DAW › Presets › Factory.
  • Register the app so double-clicking a .sfproj project opens it.

What you get

  • New Project window: name, tempo, time signature and save location in one step (⌘N).
  • Multi-track recording of microphones, guitars and audio interfaces, with clean (dry) takes and editable effects.
  • Import & Split: separate a song into vocals, guitar, bass, piano, other and drum parts (kick, snare, hi-hats, cymbals, toms), using an on-device AI model (one-click ≈ 67 MB download in the Split window) or the basic built-in splitter.
  • Generate Matching Synth: writes a pad, arpeggio or bass-synth part from your guitar and bass, with a genre-matched tone (rock, metal, pop, funk, R&B, hip-hop, electronic, ambient, jazz, folk, or auto-detect).
  • Mixer with volume faders (double-click for 0 dB), pan, mute and solo; delete tracks and stems with full undo.
  • Tone Picker and artist-style presets, a beat generator that can match your recording, and export to WAV, AIFF, FLAC, AAC, ALAC, CAF (MP3/OGG with ffmpeg), stems and MIDI.

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later. About 50 MB of disk space.
